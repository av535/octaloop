FAQ

Pricing questions.

How much does Octaloop cost?

The AI-visibility snapshot is free. The AEO audit is $750, credited toward the pilot if you start within 30 days. The 2-week pilot sprint is $3,000. Retainers are $3,500/mo (Starter) and $4,500/mo (Growth); Authority is custom-priced. PR on its own is pay-per-placement: $400 niche, $900 trade or business, $2,500 top tier, billed only when published. All prices are in USD for US clients.

Why don't you guarantee AI citations?

Nobody controls what ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews say, and anyone who guarantees it is guessing. We guarantee the deliverables we control: placements, pages and prompt tracking. If a pilot doesn't secure its 2 committed placements, we refund 25% or keep working free until it does.

How long until we see results?

The pilot runs 2 weeks, and its 2 placements are published within 30 days. We then run a before-and-after citation report on the same prompt set we measured at the start. AI answers change often and differ by engine, so we don't promise a date when you'll be cited; we show you exactly what moved and what we did.

Is there a contract, and can we cancel?

The pilot is a fixed fee with no ongoing commitment. Retainers have a 3-month minimum after the pilot, then run month-to-month. Pay-per-placement PR has no retainer at all: you're billed only when a piece is published.

How does the pilot turn into a retainer?

Start with the free snapshot, then the $750 audit (credited to the pilot if you start within 30 days) and the $3,000 pilot. Once you've seen the before-and-after report, you choose a retainer (Starter, Growth or Authority) if you want to keep going. Nothing rolls over automatically.

Should we buy PR only or AEO?

Choose PR only if you want press coverage and are happy to pay per published piece. Choose AEO if you want to show up in AI answers: it combines guaranteed placements on the sites AI tools cite with on-site work (answer-first pages, schema, crawler access) and prompt tracking, so you can see what changed.

What does the client portal show?

Your Day 0 baseline, a work log of everything we did, and week-by-week progress on placements, pages and the prompts we track for you, so you never have to ask what's happening.

What counts as a placement?

An earned mention, feature or byline about your brand on a site AI tools already cite for your category, such as a roundup, an industry or trade publication, or a relevant directory. Any sponsored placement is clearly disclosed.

Do you have pricing for India?

Yes. The prices on this page are in USD for US clients. For companies based in India, get in touch and we'll share India pricing.

Do you work with local businesses?

Yes, with a separate plan built around calls and leads, starting with a $300 local audit. See the local plans on our AEO page or get in touch.
