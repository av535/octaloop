About

A founder who has been filling rooms since 2016.

Octaloop sells sponsorships and tickets for tech conference organizers. The company is built on events Anupam Varshney has run himself.

Anupam Varshney

Anupam has run events since 2016. It started as an 8-person Bitcoin meetup in Delhi. That meetup grew into India Blockchain Tour, now in its 5th edition across 10 cities in 2026, reaching 13,000+ attendees.

He also runs Metamorphosis, a Web3 × AI summit in its 5th edition at T-Hub Hyderabad on 26–27 September 2026. Octaloop runs side events during Token2049 Singapore.

That is the work organizers are hiring: someone who has carried sponsor sales, ticket sales, and the story of an event, and who now does that as an outsourced sales and marketing team so the organizer can stay on operations.

Writing

Anupam has been a Cointelegraph contributor since 2017, with bylines in Forbes, VentureBeat, Business Insider, CoinMarketCap, and Hackernoon. Those are his bylines. They are not a list of client placements.
