Services

The sales and marketing function, handled.

Five lines of work for organizers of AI, fintech, Web3, and other tech conferences, and for the brands behind them. Sponsorships, tickets, press, AI search visibility, and the reporting that brings sponsors back. You run the event.

01

Sponsorship sales

We build the offer and then sell it. The work runs from the deck through a signed sponsor, using a network of contacts across exchanges, wallets, protocols, AI companies, and fintech companies.

  • Sponsorship deck and tier pricing
  • Prospecting and outreach
  • Negotiation and closing
  • Sponsor contacts across exchanges, wallets, protocols, AI, and fintech
02

Ticket sales and growth

Audience does not appear because a date is on a website. We run paid acquisition and the direct channels that turn a lead into a ticket.

  • Paid ads on Meta, X, Reddit, and LinkedIn
  • Lead generation
  • Email and WhatsApp campaigns
  • Community and partner promotions
03

PR and media

We pursue features and coverage in top tech, business, and crypto publications. Press is part of selling the event, not a separate announcement after the room is already full.

Founder credential, separate from client placements: Anupam Varshney has been a Cointelegraph contributor since 2017, with bylines in Forbes, VentureBeat, Business Insider, CoinMarketCap, and Hackernoon.

04

Campaign ops and reporting

The operating layer that makes the other three repeatable: what you charge, how you talk to the list, and what a sponsor receives after the event so they renew.

  • Sponsorship deck
  • Pricing strategy
  • Email and WhatsApp campaigns
  • Post-event sponsor reports that drive renewals
05

AEO / AI search visibility

Answer Engine Optimization is SEO for AI answers. We help brands and event organizers show up when people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews.

That means getting cited in the sources those models trust: editorial roundups, reviews, Wikipedia-style facts, and comparison articles. We publish facts a model can quote, earn third-party mentions, and keep the brand’s story consistent across the web.

  • Prompt mapping for the questions buyers and attendees actually ask
  • Source targeting and PR into the outlets those answers cite
  • On-site content with clear, citable product and event facts
  • Citation tracking across AI chatbots over time
