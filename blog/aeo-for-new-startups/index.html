AEO for New Startups: Getting Cited with a Brand-New Domain

Can a startup with no domain authority get cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude or Google AI Overviews? What 2026 data shows, which prompts to target first, and a 30-day plan.

By Anupam Varshney, Founder, Octaloop

Short answer: Yes, a brand-new domain can get cited by AI search: in a 2026 study of 20.5 million AI citations, 47.9% went to domains with a Domain Authority under 40, and comparison pages were cited from the weakest domains of all. The catch is that low-authority citations are narrower and less stable, so a new startup should target specific prompts it can genuinely win, publish fair comparison pages, and borrow authority through listicles, reviews, podcasts, events and press while its own domain grows.

Most AEO advice quietly assumes you already have a brand: years of backlinks, a G2 profile, some press. If you launched this year, the honest question is whether any of it applies to you. Our guide to getting a SaaS mentioned in ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews covers the full playbook. This post is the version for a founder whose domain is weeks or months old, using only published 2026 data. Octaloop doesn't have prompt-test data of its own yet, so every number below comes from the named source.

Can a brand-new startup get cited by AI search at all?

Yes, and the evidence is stronger than most founders expect.

  • Wellows analysed 20.5 million citations from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode between 8 March and 31 July 2026, across 386,000 domains. 47.9% of citations went to domains under Domain Authority (DA) 40, while the 301 domains at DA 90+ took 17.0% (Wellows, published 5 August 2026, updated 2 October 2026).
  • AirOps and Kevin Indig studied 16,851 queries and 353,799 pages in ChatGPT and found no positive link between DA or backlinks and being cited: pages that were always cited averaged DA 53, against 55.7 for pages that were never cited (The Fan-Out Effect, 13 April 2026).
  • Ahrefs looked at 75,000 brands and found that branded web mentions (correlation 0.66 to 0.71) and YouTube mentions (about 0.737) track AI visibility far more closely than backlinks or site size. ChatGPT showed the weakest link to Domain Rating, at 0.266 (Ahrefs, published December 2025, updated August 2026).

Google's own guidance points the same way. There's no special markup or "AI file" needed for AI Overviews and AI Mode; a page has to be indexed and eligible to show a snippet (Google Search Central, AI features and your website). And Conductor's analysis of 167.9 million AI Overview citations from US searches (15 June to 15 August 2026) found 57% went to pages outside the organic top 10 (Conductor, updated 25 September 2026). You don't need to outrank incumbents first.

What these studies measure is citations, not recommendations. Being a cited source and being named as "the best tool for X" are different outcomes, and we explain the difference in AEO vs GEO vs SEO.

Why does domain authority matter less on some engines than others?

Because each engine retrieves and filters differently, and the bar moves. Wellows' data shows the median DA of cited domains by engine, before and after a shift in July 2026:

Engine Median cited DA, Mar–Jun 2026 Share of citations from DA under 40, Mar–Jun Median cited DA, July 2026 What it means for a new domain
Perplexity 34 58.7% 39 Historically the most open; tightened from 23 July
ChatGPT 35 55.5% 51 Was open, then raised its bar sharply in July
Gemini 39 51.3% 38 Now the most open of the five in Wellows' data
Google AI Overviews 47 40.8% 47 Stable and stricter; ranking still helps
Google AI Mode 48 40.4% 50 Hardest for unknown brands

Source: Wellows, 20.5M citations, 8 March to 31 July 2026. Data is weighted towards US domains (76.5%).

Two other findings fill in the picture:

  • Google AI Mode rewards established brands. Ahrefs found AI Mode had the strongest correlation with branded search volume and branded anchors of the three surfaces it tested, and called it the hardest for emerging brands to crack (Ahrefs, 75k brands).
  • Claude follows Brave Search. Complete SEO traced 1,481 matched queries over ten weeks in summer 2026. 87.8% of the URLs Claude's search tool retrieved were in Brave's top 10 for the same query, against 45.6% for Google. Clients ranking 1 to 3 on Brave were cited in 92.0% of Claude's grounded answers, and those outside Brave's top 20 in 11.9% (Complete SEO, 1 September 2026). For a new domain, checking your Brave rankings is a cheap first step.

The practical lesson is that "AI search" isn't one channel. Track each engine separately, and don't judge your progress by AI Mode alone.

What's the catch for a new domain?

Low-authority citations are real, but they're narrow and fragile. Plan for that from the start.

  • One engine, not five. Of the sub-DA-40 domains Wellows tracked, 66.5% were cited by only one engine, and 4.5% by all five.
  • They fade faster. Only 44.3% of sub-DA-40 domains were still being cited two months later, against 92.0% of DA 90+ domains.
  • They show up in fewer answers. Timmermans Media ran 400 Dutch-language questions through ChatGPT in a preregistered test. 37% of the distinct sites cited had fewer than 1,000 visits a month from Google, but each appeared in 1.6 answers on average, against 9.3 for large sites, so small sites made up 15% of the citations per answer. The authors found no evidence that AI favours small sites (Timmermans Media, 8 October 2026).
  • Citations flicker. In Ahrefs' experiment, citations for the pages it tested appeared on about one in three eligible days (Ahrefs, 6 July 2026).
  • Personalised results fool you. Rankr, an agency, reported a DR 0 site that ChatGPT cited within two months on narrow scenario questions. An apparent AI Overview citation for the same site turned out to be personalisation: it appeared in 0 of 8 clean-session checks (Rankr, published 27 July 2026, updated 26 October 2026). That's one agency's case, but the lesson travels. Test in logged-out, clean sessions.

This is why early AEO results swing so much. Our post on how long AEO takes sets out realistic checkpoints, and they apply doubly to a new domain.

Which prompts can a new startup realistically win?

Narrow ones. Ahrefs launched a new conference, Ahrefs Evolve, and published pages about it. Evolve appeared in 66.4% of answers for "best SEO conferences 2026" but only 15.8% for "best marketing conferences 2026". The new brand broke into 72 prompt-engine slots where it had been absent, and 82% of those mentions came in answers that cited its own pages. For Ahrefs' established product, Brand Radar, that figure was 6% (Ahrefs, 6 July 2026). In other words, a new brand's own pages do most of the early work, and they work best on specific queries.

Wellows also found that commercial prompts cite weaker domains than informational ones, and that some of the most-cited sub-DA-40 domains are specialists: the top one earned 322,000 citations, about double the leading DA 70 to 89 publisher.

Prompt type Example for a new HR-tech startup Winnable early? Why
Broad category "best HR software" Rarely Dominated by big brands, review sites and AI Mode's preference for known names
Niche category + use case "best onboarding software for 20-person remote startups" Yes Fewer strong sources; your page can match the question closely
Comparison "[Competitor] vs [Competitor] for small teams" Yes Comparison content had the lowest median cited DA in Wellows' data (34)
Alternatives "[Incumbent] alternatives for startups in India" Yes Buyers actively look for newer options
Integration or workflow "how to sync [Tool A] with [Tool B]" Often Specific, factual, and rarely well covered
Your own brand "what is [your startup]" Must win If AI gets this wrong, fix it before anything else

Write the prompt list before you write anything else. Pick 20 to 30 prompts a buyer would type, weighted towards the bottom four rows, and run each several times per engine to get a baseline. The method is in our AEO pillar guide.

Why should comparison and alternatives pages come first?

Because they're where the authority bar is lowest. In Wellows' data, comparison content had a median cited DA of 34, and 58.8% of its citations came from domains under DA 40. How-to guides were close behind at a median of 38.

Your own site matters more than you might think for B2B software. Citlyze ran 200 buyer prompts through ChatGPT and Perplexity in August and September 2026. For B2B software prompts, brand-owned sites made up 29.1% of ChatGPT's citations and 36.7% of Perplexity's in September (Citlyze, 2 October 2026).

How to write them so they get cited, based on the 2026 research:

  1. Match the question in the heading. AirOps found pages whose headings closely matched the query were cited 41% of the time, against 29% for weak matches. Ahrefs found the titles of cited URLs were measurably closer to the prompt than those of uncited ones (Ahrefs, updated 27 August 2026).
  2. Stay focused. In the AirOps data, pages covering 26% to 50% of the sub-questions an engine fanned out to beat pages that tried to cover all of them. One comparison per page beats a 40-tool mega-list.
  3. Be fair, and say who each option suits. Ahrefs' advice from its experiment is to add yourself to lists accurately rather than crown yourself. 43% of answers that cited an Evolve page still didn't mention Evolve, so overclaiming doesn't buy you the mention.
  4. Give hard facts. Pricing, team size it suits, integrations, limits and a "last updated" date. Answer engines lift specifics, not adjectives.

How do you borrow authority you don't have yet?

Your domain will take months to earn trust, but other people's domains already have it. Getting named on them is the fastest route for a new startup, and it's the core of digital PR for AEO.

Listicles and review platforms. Overthink Group and Amadora ran 1,263 prompts across 250 niche B2B SaaS categories in June 2026. 70.8% of citations went to "best" or "top" listicles, and 51.6% of those had "2026" in the title. The G2 network took 8.0% of citations (Overthink Group, 14 July 2026). So: get a G2 or Capterra profile live with a handful of genuine reviews, then ask the authors of current, credible listicles in your niche to consider you. Expect some to charge. The same study found spam sites made up 14.5% of ChatGPT's citations and 11.3% of Perplexity's, which is a reason to be choosy, not an invitation to buy into them.

Founder-led Reddit, with care. Reddit is volatile as a citation source. Promptwatch tracked Reddit's share of ChatGPT citations falling from 3.83% (18 July to 7 August 2026) to 0.52% in mid-August, a drop it calls provisional (Axios, 20 August 2026; Semrush, 26 August 2026). Citlyze saw Reddit's share of Perplexity answers fall from 13.5% to 1.5% between August and September, and Overthink found Reddit at just 1.4% of citations for niche B2B SaaS prompts. Use Reddit to answer real questions as yourself, disclose that you're the founder, and learn how buyers describe the problem. Don't treat it as a citation hack, and never use sock-puppet accounts. The same founder voice works on LinkedIn, as we cover in founder-led marketing on LinkedIn.

Podcasts and YouTube. In Ahrefs' 75,000-brand study, YouTube mentions (in video titles, transcripts or descriptions) had the strongest correlation with AI visibility of any factor, and low-view videos didn't seem to hurt much as long as mentions were widespread. YouTube was also the single most-cited domain in AI Overviews in Overthink's B2B data, at 7.4%. Correlation isn't causation, but a founder who guests on niche podcasts that publish on YouTube with transcripts is building exactly that footprint.

Press, bylines and events. A guest byline in a trade publication or a speaker page on a conference site puts your name and category on a domain answer engines already trust. You don't need a big budget; our guide to startup PR for B2B SaaS on a budget shows how, and events and conferences as an AEO lever covers speaker pages, agendas and recaps.

What should a new startup get right on day one?

The basics that stop an engine from skipping or misdescribing you:

  • Let the crawlers in. Make sure your robots.txt and CDN don't block OAI-SearchBot, which ChatGPT search uses to find pages (OpenAI, crawler documentation), or Googlebot.
  • Make sure you're indexed. Google AI features only use indexed pages that are eligible for a snippet. Check Search Console, and look at where you rank in Brave and Bing too.
  • Publish one source of truth. An About page and an Organization schema block with your legal name, founding year, founders, location, category and pricing model. New brands are especially prone to being confused with similarly named companies. If that's already happening, our guide to fixing wrong information in ChatGPT walks through it.
  • Keep facts consistent everywhere. LinkedIn, Crunchbase, G2, your press kit and your site should say the same thing.

What should a new startup avoid?

  • Crowning yourself. "We're the best X" on your own site rarely becomes the AI's answer, and Ahrefs' experiment showed self-promotional pages can be cited without your name appearing in the answer.
  • Spam listicles and link networks. They show up in AI citations today, but they're the sort of source engines keep filtering out.
  • Sock puppets and fake reviews. They're against platform rules, and on Reddit they're easy to spot.
  • Chasing whichever source is "winning" this month. Reddit's swings in August and September 2026 show why a diversified footprint beats a single channel. Burson's Steve Rubel told Axios it's "a long game".
  • Publishing volume for its own sake. Ahrefs found almost no relationship between the number of pages on a site and AI visibility.

Should a new startup do AEO in-house or get help?

Most new startups can do the first month themselves: the baseline, the foundations and the first comparison pages. Where founders tend to stall is sustained off-site work: pitching listicle authors, podcasts and editors month after month while building the product. FAQ

Can a new website get cited by ChatGPT?

Yes. In Wellows' 2026 study of 20.5 million AI citations, 47.9% went to domains under DA 40. ChatGPT raised its bar in July 2026, though, with the median cited DA rising from 35 to 51, so target narrow, specific prompts and earn mentions on established sites as well.

Does domain authority matter for AI citations?

Less than it does for Google rankings, and differently by engine. AirOps found no positive correlation between DA and ChatGPT citations, and Ahrefs found brand mentions and YouTube mentions track AI visibility far more closely than backlinks. Google AI Overviews and AI Mode cite stronger domains than Gemini and Perplexity.

Which AI engine is easiest for a new startup?

In Wellows' July 2026 data, Gemini was the most open, with a median cited DA of 38, followed by Perplexity at 39. Google AI Mode was the hardest. For Claude, Complete SEO found citations closely track Brave Search rankings, so ranking in Brave is a practical lever.

What content should a new startup publish first for AEO?

Comparison, alternatives, use-case and integration pages that each answer one specific buyer prompt. Comparison content had the lowest median cited DA of any content type in Wellows' data, at 34.

How do startups get mentioned in AI answers without authority?

Borrow it. Get listed in credible "best" listicles and on G2 or Capterra, guest on niche podcasts that publish on YouTube, write trade bylines and speak at events. In one 2026 study of niche B2B SaaS prompts, 70.8% of citations went to listicles.

Is Reddit still worth it for AI visibility?

As a citation hack, it's unreliable. Promptwatch saw Reddit's share of ChatGPT citations fall from 3.83% to 0.52% in August 2026, and Citlyze saw a similar fall on Perplexity. As a place for founders to answer real questions openly and learn how buyers talk, it's still useful.

How long does AEO take for a new domain?

Expect early, unstable citations on narrow prompts first, and set checkpoints at day 30, 60 and 90. Sub-DA-40 domains in Wellows' data were often cited by one engine only, and fewer than half were still cited two months later, so keep building third-party mentions.
